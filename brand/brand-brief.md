## Who we are

- **Name:** Lion Roar 360 (written "Lion Roar 360" in copy, "LionRoar360" as the handle).
- **What:** Digital marketing agency. Miami-based, serving businesses nationwide, in English and Spanish.
- **Led by:** Yusbel Crespo.
- **Positioning line:** "Attract customers. Keep them connected."
- **Core promise:** Websites, SEO, Google and Meta ads, social and content built around the
  customer, plus **Lion Roar Connect** (AI CRM and automation) so every inquiry gets a next step.
- **Belief:** "Attention is only the beginning." A campaign brings someone to the site; the
  message has to make sense, the next step has to be easy, and the business needs a plan to
  respond when the inquiry arrives.
- **Method:** Understand → Build → Improve. Start with the business challenge, give every
  decision a purpose, review the data in plain language.

## Who we serve

Local and regional service businesses: home services (plumbing, builders), cabinetry and
countertops, cleaning services, aesthetics and plastic surgery practices, and any small
business that needs to be easier to find, easier to understand and easier to contact.

Named clients on the site: Jesus Cabinets & Granite, Home Service Builders, Zion Plastic
Surgery, Odalis Cleaning Services. Only reference them with the wording the site uses
(no invented results or numbers).

## Tone of voice

- Clear, calm, confident. Plain language. No hype, no jargon, no fear tactics.
- Teach first, sell second. Each video gives a business owner one useful idea they can act on.
- Honest: never promise rankings, lower ad costs or sales figures. The site itself says a
  performance score "does not guarantee" results. Keep that discipline.
- Bilingual-friendly: English narration; captions may add a short Spanish line.
